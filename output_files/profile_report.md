## 3. What `depth` means, and the cut-off frontier

- `edges.depth` equals the **source's** hop for 0/3119 edges.
- `edges.depth` equals the **destination's** hop for 2374/3119 edges.
- Interpretation used downstream: `edges.depth` is **the destination's hop**.
- The remaining **745** edges point at a destination discovered *earlier* (at a lower hop), so their `depth` is the hop at which the edge was found, not the destination's own hop. `depth` on an edge is therefore a discovery marker and is not used for any role decision; node `depth` from `nodes.parquet` is.

- MAX_DEPTH read from the data: **4**.
- Nodes at depth 4: **444**, of which **0** do have outgoing edges.
- Consequence: for depth-4 nodes outflow is **unknown, not zero**. `terminal` is never assigned there (guideline §8.2).
